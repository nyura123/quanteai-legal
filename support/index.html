Common questions

How do I create an account?
Tap Sign up on the welcome screen, or choose Create account on the sign-in screen. You can also explore QuantE AI as a guest without an account.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Sign in, open Settings, and use the subscription management section, or email us and we'll help.

How do I delete my account and data?
Sign in, open Settings, and choose Delete Account. You'll be asked to type DELETE to confirm, and your account and associated data are permanently removed. If you have a subscription, cancel it in Settings first. If you can't sign in, email us from the address associated with your account and we'll delete it for you. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Is QuantE AI financial advice?
No. QuantE AI is an informational and educational tool. It does not execute real trades or provide personalized financial advice.
