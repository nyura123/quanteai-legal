Common questions

Can I create an account in the iOS app?
Account creation isn't available in the iOS app. You can explore QuantE AI as a guest, or sign in with an existing account.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Sign in, open Settings, and use the subscription management section, or email us and we'll help.

How do I delete my account and data?
Email us from the address associated with your account and we'll delete your account and associated data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Is QuantE AI financial advice?
No. QuantE AI is an informational and educational tool. It does not execute real trades or provide personalized financial advice.
