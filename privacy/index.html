How we use information

  • To provide the Service: answering your questions, showing market data, tracking your portfolio, and running simulations.
  • To personalize AI insights using the portfolio and preference data you provide.
  • To manage your account and subscription.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot problems.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To improve the Service using aggregated or de-identified data.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it to track you across other companies' apps and websites. The Service contains no third-party advertising.

Third-party AI services

AI features are powered by third-party models. When you use them, we send your chat messages and, if you are signed in, the portfolio and preference data needed for the request to OpenRouter, which routes the request to the AI model provider that generates the response. These providers process the data to return a response, under their own terms and privacy policies. The app asks for your permission before any of this data is shared.

Please don't include sensitive information, such as bank account numbers, passwords, or government ID numbers, in chat messages.

Data retention

We keep account, chat, and portfolio data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify your personal information within 30 days, except where we must keep certain records (for example, payment records) to meet legal, tax, or accounting obligations. Copies in backups are overwritten on a rolling basis within 90 days.
